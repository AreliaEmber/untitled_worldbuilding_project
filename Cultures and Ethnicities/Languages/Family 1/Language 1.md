# Phonotactics:
Rules:
- Base structure up to (C)(V)(C)
- Onset:
	- Disallow all nasal consonants (m, n, ng)
- Nucleus:
	- All monophthongs allowed, diphthongs include: ai, ao, oi, ei, ae
- Coda:
	- Disallow all approximants (w, r)
	- Disallow all glottal sounds (', h)
- Allow glottal stop (') only between vowels (i.e. cannot be at the start of a word, the previous syllable cannot have a coda)
- Simple base structure makes clustering constraints mostly irrelevant
Disambiguation of the romanization system:
- to tell the difference between (ng) and (n g) at syllable boundaries, the symbol for a glottal stop (') should be placed between (n) and (g) -> (n'g) to symbolize that they should be pronounced separately
# Syntax:
- Base word order: SOV
- Adjectives come before nouns they modify
- Adpositions primarily in form of postpositions - rare/essentially non-existent at this stage, as position is denoted mostly by case
- Possession used as an adjective
- Adverbs/auxiliaries come after verbs
# Grammar:
- Plural conferred with an adjective
- tense: Present unmarked, past and future marked. Further tenses achieved with marked modal verbs.
- progressive unmarked, or marked with an adverb when needed
- habitual present unmarked, or marked with an adverb when needed
- passive is expressed through transitive-intransitive pairs
- nominative-accusative language (marks the object of transitive verbs)
- causative uses auxilliary verb ("to cause")
