The age of colonization began in the year 1772 AR with the first establishment of a colony on [[Continent 4]] by state funded settlers from [[Continent 1]]. The early successes of this colony resulted in a before unseen fervour amongst leaders in states on [[Continent 1]] and [[Continent 2]] (and to a lesser extend [[Continent 3]]) to set up intercontinental empires and stake their claim to the vast lands of [[Continent 4]]. This was not popular amongst the native inhabitants.

The age ended in the year 2003 AR as the first of a series of major, bloody conflicts spanning most of the planet erupted, giving way to [[The Age of Conflicts]].
