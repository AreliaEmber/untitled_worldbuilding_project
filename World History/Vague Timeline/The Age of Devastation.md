The age of Devastation followed the end of the seven great wars, beginning in 2197 AR. Despite the name, it was a period of relative stability and growing populations, as the major powers of the globe had been so devastated by almost two centuries of conflict that the majority were unable to continue pursuing international interests. Many nations fractured in this period as the infrastructure holding them together had been torn down in prior conflicts.

The end of this age is marked by the first true contact with creatures from another planet in the year 2355 AR, marking the beginning of [[The Age of Spaceflight]].
