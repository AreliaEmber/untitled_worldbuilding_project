The largest of the [[Draconia]], an adult dragon can weight anywhere from 3 to 50 tons. While their growth does slow with time, it cannot be said that a dragon ever truly stops growing. Primary mundane features of the dragon include their strong jaws and talons, hard scales, and large wings, although these wings are incapable of flight through mundane means. 

While they are primarily carnivorous, most dragons also enjoy eating various types of [[Mana Crystals]], and as such they have evolved to use mana from dissolved crystals for various purposes, including but not limited to:
- creating enough upward force for their otherwise too small wings to sustain flight
- generating additional oxygen to aid in respiration
- igniting said oxygen when expelled, effectively letting the dragon exhale a column of fire to cook its prey, allowing for more efficient digestion.

Dragons are native primarily to the highland regions of [[Continent 1]] and [[Continent 2]], and can only be rarely found soaring around the peaks in the north of [[Continent 3]].
